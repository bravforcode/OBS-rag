# Agent Crew — Core Agents

This file describes the 8 core agents in your vault. Each agent is specialized for a specific role in helping you manage your knowledge.

---

## 1. Architect

**Role**: Vault structure, governance, and onboarding.

**Responsibilities**:
- Vault initialization and setup
- Folder structure and area management
- Templates and naming conventions
- Long-term vault evolution

---

## 2. Scribe

**Role**: Capture and refine notes from raw input.

**Responsibilities**:
- Save quick thoughts, ideas, voice notes
- Refine unstructured text into proper notes
- Create initial note structure

---

## 3. Sorter

**Role**: Triage inbox and file notes to proper locations.

**Responsibilities**:
- Batch-sort inbox items
- Route notes to correct areas
- Update MOCs and indices

---

## 4. Seeker

**Role**: Search and retrieve information from the vault.

**Responsibilities**:
- Find notes by keyword or topic
- Answer questions based on vault content
- Generate timelines, comparisons, summaries

---

## 5. Connector

**Role**: Discover and strengthen links between notes.

**Responsibilities**:
- Find orphan notes and cross-area connections
- Suggest new links between ideas
- Analyze knowledge graph health

---

## 6. Librarian

**Role**: Vault health, quality, and consistency.

**Responsibilities**:
- Weekly vault audits
- Detect duplicates and broken links
- Track consistency and tag hygiene
- Generate health reports

---

## 7. Transcriber

**Role**: Process audio, recordings, and transcripts.

**Responsibilities**:
- Transcribe audio files
- Create structured notes from recordings
- Extract decisions and action items
- Process podcast summaries

---

## 8. Postman

**Role**: Email and calendar integration.

**Responsibilities**:
- Scan inbox for actionable emails
- Import calendar events
- Create meeting prep notes
- Manage integrations (Gmail, Google Calendar)
