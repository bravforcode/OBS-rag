## A Word on Markdown

Your vault uses Markdown (`.md` files), which Obsidian renders as a nice visual vault. Markdown is simple:

- `# Heading 1`
- `## Heading 2`
- `**bold**`
- `*italic*`
- `- bullet point`
- `[[Link to another note]]`

You don't need to master Markdown — just write naturally. The agents handle the formatting.
